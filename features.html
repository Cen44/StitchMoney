Features

Money tools that work before checkout, not after regret.

These feature ideas extend the Stitch Money consumer experience into planning, routing, support, and privacy.

R

Fee-first checkout

Compare bank, card, Pay Later, and crypto costs before confirming a payment.

%

Rewards optimiser

Choose the best route for reward value, lowest fee, or fastest settlement.

B

Budget lock

Set category guardrails that warn you before spending breaks your plan.

?

Dispute timeline

Track refunds, failed payments, and merchant replies with clear status updates.

S

Spendable balance

Show what is actually available after bills, repayments, and saving goals.

P

Privacy mode

Hide balances and sensitive amounts instantly when using the app in public.
